What is Trigonometry?

Trigonometry studies relationships between the sides and angles of triangles. It originated in the Hellenistic world for astronomical studies.

Visualizing Trigonometry

To begin understanding Trigonometry we need to understand the main ideas of a triangle. The 3 different sides are called the Hypotenuse, Opposite and Adjacent. The Opposite angle and Adjacent angle change depending on where the angle you are trying to find is.

SohCahToa


SohCahToa is a device we use to remember what trigonometric ratio to use. Either: Sin, Cos or Tan.

Simply Sin is Opposite over Hypotenuse, Cos is Adjacent over Hypotenuse and Tan is Opposite over Adjacent.

Getting an answer


Now that we know what Trigonometry is and how to use SohCahToa we can now find the answer to the angle we are looking for.

We can use the formula:

sin(θ) = Opposite / Hypotenuse
cos(θ) = Adjacent / Hypotenuse
tan(θ) = Opposite / Adjacent

Example Questions


Now that we know how to use Trigonometry we can now answer some example questions.


Example 1:

Question: Find the angle of a triangle with an Opposite of 5 and a Hypotenuse of 13.


Answer: sin(θ) = 5 / 13

Example 2:

Question: Find the angle of a triangle with an Adjacent of 7 and a Hypotenuse of 25.


Answer: cos(θ) = 7 / 25
